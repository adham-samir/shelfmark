## Stage 1 coverage

Every source requirement above has a journey, data dependency, and acceptance/evidence path in the SRS. Functional modules remain unimplemented. Requirements blocked by business interpretation or policy decisions are labeled rather than silently resolved.

## Stage 2 design baseline and Stage 3 start

The accepted stack and business policies are recorded in `docs/decisions.md`. Stage 2 provides the complete relational baseline, MongoDB catalog validator/indexes, seeds, UI/flow specification, and catalog/platform API contracts. See [data architecture](architecture/data-foundation.md), [UI/UX](ui-ux/README.md), and [API contracts](api/README.md).

Stage 3 verified catalog/commerce foundations, SF-01 through SF-03 interfaces, the complete customer storefront, and BO-01 inventory, BO-02 pricing, BO-03 RFM segmentation, BO-04 trained fraud detection, and BI-01 analytics with their back-office surfaces. Stage 4 mapped every requirement above to a result and reproducible evidence in [the test report](test-report.md), including the DEC-014 workload and the WCAG 2.2 AA automated gate. The demo identity harness (COM-07, DEC-017) adds customer registration/sign-in/account/orders and a separately gated operator back office; it is a labelled demonstration boundary, not a production identity provider. The operator order lifecycle (COM-08/COM-09, DEC-018) adds a read queue plus fulfillment, cancellation, refund, and stock-adjustment actions, each attributed to the acting operator. Real OpenAI provider calls and cloud placement remain external dependencies; no requirement row claims them.
